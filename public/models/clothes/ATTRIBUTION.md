# Clothing assets

## Active try-on

Catalog uses **real product GLBs** from `ready/` (puffer, hoodie, tees, pants).

Fitted with `AvatarFit` + `garmentFit` (scale + place on the body). These are
static display meshes (often A-pose), not Mixamo-skinned — sleeves may not
match a T-pose avatar perfectly, but they are real garments, not placeholders.

The `tpose/` folder holds experimental primitives and is **not** used in the catalog.
